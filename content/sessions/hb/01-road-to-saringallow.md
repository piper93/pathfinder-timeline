## Session Recap

The party escorted Ethran to Saringallow, encountering hostile loyalists and undead along the way. Upon meeting the local Hellbreakers lieutenants, they investigated a missing member, Gertraud, and successfully rescued her from a warehouse occupied by loyalists.

## Key Events

- Maric won the Competition of Tales with a tap-dancing performance about the Goblinblood Wars, earning the party the finest ale and two healing potions.
- A new decree offering rewards for turning in dissenters and revolutionaries was posted outside the town hall. The party departed for Saringallow.
- The party fought four loyalists whilst gathering supplies in a small village. Maric attempted to disguise the incident as an accident involving marbles.
- Velius dispatched two undead bats by the river. The ferryman revealed that the dead had become restless since "god stuff" fell from the sky.
- The party met the local Hellbreakers lieutenants and rescued Gertraud from three loyalists holding her captive in the League's warehouse.
