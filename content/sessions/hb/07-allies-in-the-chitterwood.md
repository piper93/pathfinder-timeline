## Session Recap

The party ventured into the Chitterwood in search of Viro Ahala, encountering a local goblin community willing to aid their investigation. After dealing with the Isgeri Slayers, they learned that Ahala's ritual had disturbed three goblin sites of power, which must be cleansed before his location can be revealed.

## Key Events

- The party returned to Vordia before travelling to Umok, where they gathered information to narrow down Viro Ahala's location.
- Following tracks through the Chitterwood, the party discovered a petrified tree stump guarded by three ort devils. Maric defeated one of them with a single attack using salt.
- After defeating the devils, the party encountered a goblin Looksee party led by Zagnub, who escorted them to Thorngrip and introduced them to the community's elder, Brakknap.
- In exchange for Brakknap's assistance, the party tracked down and killed the Isgeri Slayers, a group responsible for hunting and murdering goblins in the forest.
- Brakknap revealed that Ahala's ritual had disturbed three goblin sites of power, preventing the ancestors from locating him. He asked the party to cleanse these sites so that the ancestors could reveal Ahala's whereabouts.
