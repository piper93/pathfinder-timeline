## Session Recap

The party cleansed the three goblin sites of power, allowing Brakknap to reveal Ahala's location within the Stagfall Caves. Venturing into the caves, they encountered devils, Hellknights and a basilisk that temporarily turned Velius to stone.

## Key Events

- At the Little Tree, the party encountered distorted magical energy that caused Zinx to shrink. After Velius defeated a bear, Zinx dispersed the magic and restored the site.
- Maric successfully dispelled the magic at the Hungry Hole and Moss Pond. At the latter, the party also fought several regenerating moss sloths, eventually overcoming their regeneration using Alchemist's Fire.
- Having cleansed all three sites, the party returned to Brakknap, who revealed that Ahala was performing his ritual in the Stagfall Caves and allowed them to keep his pendant.
- The party entered the Stagfall Caves, fighting devils and encountering Hellknights as they explored the tunnels. Velius landed several critical hits, whilst Maric sabotaged the Hellknights' belongings to make them look foolish.
- The party encountered a basilisk that turned Velius to stone. After defeating the creature, they followed Zinx's advice and used Maric's cookware to collect its blood, pouring it over Velius to reverse the petrification.
