## Session Recap

After encountering a contingent of Hellknights at Wolfpoint Garrison, the party returned to Vordia to investigate the Lucent Mission. Fighting their way through the Sisters of the Golden Erinyes, they uncovered further details of Ahala's planned ritual in the Chitterwood.

## Key Events

- The party arrived at Wolfpoint Garrison and met Caeto Vulaunex, a Hellknight travelling towards Elidir who had already resolved the troubles at the garrison.
- Returning to Vordia, the party travelled into the mountains to investigate the Lucent Mission. After defeating the sisters guarding the entrance, they discovered a concealed statue of Asmodeus within the pulpit.
- The party fought their way through the mission, encountering hostile sisters and devils. Maric's attempt to pass himself off as one of the sisters using his stolen robes only made him a more appealing target.
- After defeating the remaining sisters and a summoned devil, the party secured the surrender of the Mother Superior.
- The Mother Superior revealed that Ahala planned to perform a ritual in the Chitterwood, summoning a devil he could control without making a deal, intending to rid Isger of its "goblin problems". The party spared her life and left her with the knowledge that Ahala had deceived her.
