## Session Recap

After returning to headquarters, the party learned of Rhokrag's disappearance and tracked him to the Waterspout Inn. Following a confrontation with loyalists and Chelaxian marines, they rescued Rhokrag and learned that a Hellknight was seeking information on the League.

## Key Events

- The party recovered from their encounter with the snakes and discovered a wagon left by the rescued crew as a gesture of thanks.
- Upon returning to headquarters, the party learned that Rhokrag had gone missing whilst investigating whether the Hellknights had regrouped.
- The party investigated the Waterspout Inn, where a bar brawl with loyalists escalated into a larger fight involving reinforcements and Chelaxian marines.
- Aboard the docked ship, the party discovered Rhokrag being interrogated by the captain and a summoned devil. They defeated both and freed Rhokrag, who warned them that Hellknight Ahala was offering payment for information on the League.
- At a subsequent League meeting, the party were assigned an area of Isger in which to provide aid to locals and improve the League's reputation.
