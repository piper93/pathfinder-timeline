## Session Recap

The party continued their efforts to improve the Hellbreakers League's reputation, investigating forest fires near Umok and resolving a dispute between Stumpfield and Fernsby. Whilst travelling towards Wolfpoint Garrison, they encountered Sister Sylve, who revealed Viro Ahala's plans to summon a devil.

## Key Events

- Further investigation of the Vordia orphanage uncovered a draft letter stating that the orphans had been sent elsewhere. The party shared their findings with the townsfolk before departing for Umok.
- In Umok, the party encountered an injured goblin named Grizzo, whom the townsfolk suspected of causing the recent forest fires. The party investigated the Chitterwood and defeated two hell hounds, returning with evidence of Grizzo's innocence.
- After participating in Stumpfield's cooperative art day, the party were asked to investigate accusations from neighbouring Fernsby.
- The party discovered that Hissbite, a goblin snake, was responsible for repeatedly destroying Fernsby's village green. After burying the snakes killed during their investigation, they brought Hissbite to Stumpfield in the hope that he could find a community there.
- Whilst travelling towards Wolfpoint Garrison, the party encountered Sister Sylve of the Sisters of the Golden Erinyes. She revealed that Viro Ahala was impersonating a Hellknight and planning a ritual to summon a devil, directing the party towards the Lucent Mission for further answers.
