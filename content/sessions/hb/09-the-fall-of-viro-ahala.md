## Session Recap

The party confronted Viro Ahala in the Stagfall Caves, disrupting his ritual and putting an end to his plans. After celebrating their victory with the goblins of Thorngrip, they returned to Saringallow to learn that the League's efforts had successfully improved its reputation throughout the countryside.

## Key Events

- Continuing through the Stagfall Caves, the party were ambushed by Hellknights and devils. Velius swiftly defeated them, although the last Hellknight managed to shout a warning deeper into the caves.
- The party discovered Viro Ahala performing his ritual. Their intervention destabilised the ritual's magical energies, causing chaos throughout the ensuing fight.
- Delilah intercepted a critical hit intended for Maric, leaving her dying. After healing her, Maric successfully dispelled the ritual energy, leaving Ahala staggered and allowing Velius to kill him.
- The party returned to Thorngrip, where the goblins celebrated their victory with costumes and plenty of pickle liquor. Maric reconsidered his dislike of goblins, and Brakknap gifted the party his crystal as a symbol of friendship between Thorngrip and the League.
- Returning to Saringallow, the party learned that the League's efforts had significantly improved public sentiment towards them throughout the countryside. They were assigned local League work whilst awaiting further instructions from the founders.
