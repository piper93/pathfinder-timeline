## Session Recap

The party travelled to Vordia, where rumours of strange activity led them to investigate a seemingly abandoned orphanage. After several encounters with troublesome haunts, they discovered and defeated an Attic Whisperer.

## Key Events

- During the journey to Vordia, Maric performed some anti-Cheliax slam poetry at an inn, which was poorly received by the audience.
- Upon arriving in the heavily pro-Cheliax town of Vordia, the party heard concerning rumours about the local orphanage. Town records indicated that its two remaining orphans had recently been adopted, but the family names were unreadable.
- The party investigated the orphanage, discovering a haunted toybox that opened into the void. They managed to hold it shut whilst Zinx dispersed the haunt.
- A second haunt attacked the party with flying spaghetti, knocking Zinx unconscious before Delilah managed to calm the spirits by telling them off. The party retreated for the day, contemplating burning down the orphanage.
- Returning the following morning, the party discovered an Attic Whisperer upstairs. The creature stole Delilah's voice and knocked Velius unconscious by stealing his breath, before Zinx finally defeated it with a single punch.
