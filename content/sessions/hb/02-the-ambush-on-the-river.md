## Session Recap

Tasked with securing supplies for the Hellbreakers League, the party ambushed a Chelaxian ship. Their mission took a disastrous turn when the ship began to sink, followed by an encounter with venomous snakes that left Maric as the only conscious member of the party.

## Key Events

- The party learned of the Hellbreakers League's plans to replenish their supplies and were tasked with intercepting a Chelaxian ship.
- The party ambushed a passing Chelaxian ship by leaping aboard, with every member landing prone on the deck. Most of the crew surrendered, but two swabbies and the captain resisted.
- After the captain was knocked unconscious, a failsafe triggered explosions below deck. As the ship began to sink, the party worked together to rescue the crew, secure supplies and reach safety. Delilah brought the unconscious captain ashore.
- Whilst recovering on the riverbank, the party was attacked by two venomous snakes disturbed by the debris. Zinx, Delilah and Velius fell unconscious from the effects of their venom.
- Unable to wake his companions and concerned about the captain regaining consciousness, Maric quietly rolled the unconscious captain into the river to drown.
