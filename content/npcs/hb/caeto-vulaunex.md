Caeto Vulaunex is a Hellknight Paralictor of the Order of the Godclaw, travelling through Isger towards the capital, Elidir. The party encountered him at Wolfpoint Garrison, where he had already resolved the local troubles and expressed his appreciation for their interest in maintaining law and order in the countryside.
